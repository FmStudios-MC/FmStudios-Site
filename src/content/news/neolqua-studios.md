FabiMvurice Interactive is now **Neolqua Studios**. Same two dudes, same projects, a new name.

The new logo is a single ribbon **N** set in a drop. The colours come straight from the water: Lagoon, Neo Aqua and Abyss.

## What changed

- A new name across the whole site.
- A new look on every page, built like a tiled pool: the tiles are the block grid, the water is the drop.
- The Create Fly ports for Minecraft 26.2 are still first on the home page, with direct links to Modrinth and CurseForge.
- Every project still shows its Minecraft version, loader and status before you download.

## What stays the same

The projects, the download pages on Modrinth and CurseForge, and this website's address. Releases, updates and questions are still on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/x9jsed8qyR).
