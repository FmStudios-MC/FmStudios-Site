FabiMvurice Interactive has a new logo: a block with our name carved into it. In Minecraft everything starts as a block, and so does every mod. The **F** is carved into the left face, the **M** into the right, and the brass top is the lit side.

The website has been rebuilt around that block. It uses the same three colours as the logo: Forge Ink, Brass and Paper. Every project now sits on its own block, and on the home page you can see what is on the bench right now.

## What changed

- A new look across every page, built from the logo's isometric block.
- The home page now shows the Create Fly ports for Minecraft 26.2 first, with direct links to Modrinth and CurseForge.
- Project pages show the Minecraft version, loader and status in one place before you download.
- The Halloween theme is gone for now. If a seasonal look comes back, it will be made for the new design.
- Under the hood, the site runs on the latest Astro and dependencies.

Older news posts have been taken down with the rework. Releases, updates and questions are still on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/x9jsed8qyR).
