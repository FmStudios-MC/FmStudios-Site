## What Is This Pack?
 
Create Unbound is a modpack built around one idea: **Create should be the whole game, not a side activity.** Instead of throwing in a dozen unrelated tech mods, this pack takes Create and stacks nearly every good addon on top of it. Industry, trains, decoration, food, economy... so everything you build stays in the same visual and mechanical language.
 
The pack is tuned to actually run well. Sodium, Lithium, ModernFix and friends are included and configured, and shaders are already installed. You just need to turn them on.
 
![Screenshot of a Create Unbound factory build with machinery and contraptions](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1354764/description_882211b4-d15b-4fbe-bf71-25c10e5dab03.png)
 
***
 
##  Built Around Create
 
Create 6 is the backbone of the pack, extended by a curated set of addons that add depth without breaking the feel of the base mod:
 
*   **Create: Connected** — quality-of-life blocks and fixes the base mod is missing
*   **Create: Copycats+** — turn any block into any shape
*   **Create Deco, Create Encased** — industrial building blocks that match Create's palette
*   **Create: Dragons Plus** — more parts, more colours, more options
*   **Create Pattern Schematics**  — save, share and print your builds
*   **Create Steam´n Rails** — all about building trains
**![Screenshot showing Create addon blocks and decoration pieces in use](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1354764/description_82a08e8d-df55-4bce-a2d8-0b2b40f15824.png)**
 
***
 
## Take to the Skies
 
Rails are not the only way to move. **Create: Aeronautics** adds physics-driven flight to the pack. Nothing here is a prefab vehicle: you build an airframe out of blocks, bolt on engines, propellers and control surfaces, and then fly the thing yourself.
 
*   **Real flight physics** — lift, drag and weight all matter, so the shape you build is the shape that flies
*   **Engines, propellers and control surfaces** — planes, helicopters or whatever else you can make hold together
*   **Hands-on cockpits** — take the controls instead of watching a contraption move on its own
***
 
## Trains, Rails & Logistics
 
Create's train system gets the full treatment here. Build a line between your bases, then build a line between everyone's bases:
 
*   **Create Railways Navigator** — a real timetable and route-planning UI, like an actual rail network
*   **Create: Blocks & Bogies** and **Create Train Parts** — far more rolling stock and track pieces
*   **Pantographs and Wires** — overhead catenary lines for electric trains
*   **Train Utilities** — signals, stations and operational tooling
*   **Create Threaded Trains** — keeps large networks from eating your tick rate
**![Screenshot of a Create train network with tracks and rolling stock](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1354764/description_32203998-2598-4744-9946-52f8b20afbe9.png)**

## Farming & Food
 
**Farmer's Delight** brings real cooking, and **Create: Central Kitchen** plus **Create: Cheese Factory** let you automate all of it.  From crops in the field to finished meals in a barrel. **Right Click Harvest** and **AppleSkin** keep the day-to-day farming painless.

## Performance & Visuals
 
Performance was treated as a feature, not an afterthought. The pack includes **Sodium**, **Lithium**, **FerriteCore**, **ModernFix**, **BadOptimizations**, **Entity Culling**, **Immersive Optimization**, **Create Better FPS** and **Create Threaded Trains**, all pre-configured.
 
**Shaders are already installed.** **Iris** ships with **Complementary Reimagined**. Open Video Settings, enable the shader pack, and you are done. No extra downloads, no setup.
 
A custom main menu and loading screen round the whole thing off.

## FAQ
 
 
**Do I need to install shaders myself?**  
No. Shaders are already included.
 
**Can I play this on a server?**  
Yes. A server pack is provided on the Files tab, and the pack ships with claims, teams and voice chat ready to go. Also you can join our official community server by clicking the button on the multiplayer page.
 
**My game is stuttering / crashing on startup.**  
Make sure you are on Java 21 and have at least 4 GB allocated, and update your graphics drivers. If it still fails, post the full `latest.log` and `crash-report` in the comments or on Discord. Screenshots of the error alone are rarely enough to help.
 
**Can I make a video or stream this pack?**  
Absolutely, and please do. A link back to the CurseForge page is appreciated.
 
**Can I add mods / use this in my own pack?**  
Add whatever you like for your own play. For public re-uploads or derivative packs, please ask first.
